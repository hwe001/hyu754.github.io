Segments II+III (left lateral lobe) — the graft used in adult-to-infant living-donor transplantation.

Method: the portal-vein skeleton is traced from the main trunk to find the branches topologically confirmed to supply segments II/III — 6 real terminals, not just ones spatially nearby. Each is extended by OpenCCO (Kerautret et al. 2023), a published Constrained Constructive Optimization implementation, grown within that branch's own perfusion territory (confined to the segment volume). Radii are computed by OpenCCO's own Poiseuille/Murray's-law model, not assumed.
